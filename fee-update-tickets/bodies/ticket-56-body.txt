## Overview
Amends the refund calculation logic defined in ticket #52 (missed contribution escalation and group removal). The original formula deducted a 2% processing fee from the removed member's refund. This has been removed - the refund is now simply the member's total contributions minus their outstanding debt, with no additional deduction.

## What changes

### Original formula (ticket #52)
```
refund_amount = total_contributions_made - outstanding_debt - processing_fee (2%)
```

### Corrected formula (this ticket)
```
refund_amount = total_contributions_made - outstanding_debt
```

## Rationale
The removed member has already paid the platform fee on every contribution they made (ticket #54). Deducting an additional 2% processing fee on exit would mean penalising an honest contributor twice - once for each contribution they made in good faith, and again when the platform is returning their own money. The contribution fee already funds the reserve and platform operations. No further deduction is justified.
