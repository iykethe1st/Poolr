## Overview
Define and implement the full escalation path for a member who misses a contribution and fails to clear their debt within the grace window. This ticket covers the group removal flow, the corrected net-position-based decision tree, and the automated refund scheduling for net-positive removed members. It sits between the penalty engine (ticket #10) and the ban system (ticket #48).

## Core design principle
The decision tree after grace window expiry is driven entirely by the member's net financial position relative to the group - not by an arbitrary admin decision. This keeps the system fair, fully automated, and free of human bias.

## Corrected decision tree

### Net position definition
```
net_position = total_payouts_received - total_contributions_made
```
- Positive net_position = member has taken out more than they put in (profited)
- Negative net_position = member has put in more than they taken out (owed money back)
- Zero net_position = member has made no contributions and received no payout

### After grace window expires (debt not cleared)

```
net_position > 0 (received payout exceeding contributions):
  - BANNED (handled by ticket #48)
  - Group cash flow is impacted - redistribution or replacement required
  - No refund

net_position <= 0 (contributed more than received, including zero payout received):
  - REMOVED_DEFAULT (not banned, can still log in)
  - Group cash flow is NOT impacted - no redistribution, no replacement needed
  - Group rotation schedule is completely unchanged for all other members
  - Net refund scheduled for removed member's original payout date
  - Everything automated - no admin decision required
```

### Why no redistribution for net-positive removed members
When a net-negative member (net_position <= 0) is removed, their payout slot on their original payout date is still honoured - but as a refund to them rather than a full group payout. The group's other members' dates, amounts, and rotation positions are completely unaffected because:
- The removed member's future contributions will not come in
- But their full payout will also not go out to them
- The reserve covers only the net refund amount (contributions minus debt minus fee) on the original payout date
- The group pot for that date remains intact for future cycles

Redistribution and replacement are ONLY triggered for net-positive removed members (handled by ticket #48's ban flow), where the group is genuinely short going forward.

## Net refund calculation

```
refund_amount = total_contributions_made - outstanding_debt
```

- If refund_amount > 0: credit to removed member's Poolr Wallet on their original payout date, funded by the platform reserve
- If refund_amount <= 0: no refund, outstanding balance persists, member is notified of remaining debt
- The reserve is replenished when the member eventually clears their debt

## Escalation path in full

### Single missed contribution
- Contribution marked MISSED (day after due date)
- Flat penalty fee applied immediately
- `Debt` record created (ticket #47)
- Platform reserve covers shortfall - recipient's payout still goes out on time (ticket #50)
- All group members notified by name who missed and that the payout is unaffected
- Grace window opens: 7 days to clear debt before escalation
- Reminders sent to defaulter on grace day 1, 3, and 6

### After grace window expires (debt not cleared)
- Calculate net_position for the member
- If net_position > 0: trigger ban via ticket #48
- If net_position <= 0: trigger group removal (this ticket)

### Group removal (net_position <= 0)
- Member status set to REMOVED_DEFAULT
- Calculate refund_amount
- If refund_amount > 0: schedule BullMQ refund job for their original payout date
- If refund_amount <= 0: no refund job, debt persists
- No admin action required - fully automated
- Trust score penalised (-20 for removal, on top of -15 for the missed contribution)
- Removed member CAN still log in but cannot join any new group until debt is cleared

### Repeat missed contributions (2+ misses, no prior removal)
- Second miss in same group: grace window shortened to 3 days
- Third miss in same group: immediate removal, no grace window, trust score -30

## Schema changes

### Update `GroupMember` model
- `member_status` enum: add `REMOVED_DEFAULT` to existing values
- Add `removed_at` (timestamp, nullable)
- Add `removal_reason` (string, nullable)
- Add `refund_amount` (int, nullable) -- calculated net refund, null if not eligible
- Add `refund_scheduled_date` (date, nullable) -- the original payout date the refund will be credited
- Add `refund_credited_at` (timestamp, nullable) -- set when refund is actually credited

### No VacatedSlot table needed
The previous design included a `VacatedSlot` table and an admin 48hr decision window. This is removed entirely for net-negative removals. The group schedule is unchanged and no admin decision is required. The `VacatedSlot` concept only applies to bans (net-positive removals, handled in ticket #48).

## Tasks

### Escalation processor
- [ ] Create `DefaultEscalationProcessor` BullMQ processor
- [ ] On grace window expiry job: calculate net_position
- [ ] Route to `BanService.banUser()` (ticket #48) if net_position > 0
- [ ] Route to `GroupsService.removeMemberForDefault()` if net_position <= 0
- [ ] Track miss count per member per group for shortened grace windows on repeat misses
- [ ] Third miss: enqueue immediate removal job, bypass grace window

### Group removal flow
- [ ] `GroupsService.removeMemberForDefault(groupId, memberId)`:
  - Set `GroupMember.member_status` to `REMOVED_DEFAULT`
  - Calculate `refund_amount` = total_contributions - outstanding_debt
  - If refund_amount > 0: store on `GroupMember`, enqueue BullMQ refund job for original payout date
  - If refund_amount <= 0: store zero, no refund job
  - All within a single Prisma `$transaction`
- [ ] `GroupsService.join()` guard: block REMOVED_DEFAULT members with outstanding debt from joining any new group

### Refund processor
- [ ] Create `RefundProcessor` BullMQ processor
- [ ] On refund job execution (original payout date):
  - Call `ReserveService.drawdown(refundAmount, debtId)` to fund the refund
  - Call `WalletService.credit(userId, refundAmount, reference)` to credit removed member's wallet
  - Set `GroupMember.refund_credited_at`
  - All within a single Prisma `$transaction`
- [ ] After each cycle contribution is recorded: re-evaluate any pending refund jobs to check if refund_amount calculation has changed (edge case: if debt is partially paid before payout date, recalculate)

### PayoutProcessor update
- [ ] On each cycle payout date, check if the recipient is REMOVED_DEFAULT
- [ ] If yes and refund job is scheduled: confirm refund job will fire (do not send normal group payout to this member)
- [ ] If yes and no refund job (refund_amount <= 0): log and skip, no payment
- [ ] If no (normal active member): proceed with normal payout flow

### Notifications
- [ ] On removal: notify removed member (push + email):
  - "You have been removed from [Group Name]."
  - If refund > 0: "Your net contributions of NGN [refund_amount] will be returned to your Poolr Wallet on [original_payout_date]. Outstanding debt of NGN [debt] has been deducted."
  - If refund <= 0: "After deducting your outstanding debt of NGN [debt], no refund is due. You still owe NGN [remaining_balance]."
- [ ] On removal: notify group admin (in-app only): "[Member Name] has been removed. Your group's payout schedule is unchanged."
- [ ] On removal: notify remaining group members (in-app): "[Member Name] has been removed from the group. Your payout dates and amounts are unaffected."
- [ ] On refund credited: notify removed member (push + in-app): "NGN [refund_amount] has been credited to your Poolr Wallet as a refund of your contributions to [Group Name]."

### Admin visibility
- [ ] `GET /admin/groups/:id/removed-members` -- list removed members with net position, refund amount, and refund status
- [ ] Show removed member count and pending refunds in group detail (ticket #13)

## Acceptance criteria
- A net-negative removed member's group has zero changes to any other member's payout date, rotation position, or payout amount
- Refund is credited to the removed member's Poolr Wallet on their exact original payout date, not before and not after
- A removed member with outstanding debt cannot join any new group until debt is cleared
- If refund_amount <= 0, no wallet credit occurs and the outstanding balance is clearly communicated to the member
- All removal, refund calculation, and wallet credit steps are atomic (Prisma $transaction) - no partial states
- The PayoutProcessor correctly skips the normal group payout for REMOVED_DEFAULT members and defers to the RefundProcessor
- Removed member can still log into the app and see their refund scheduled date and amount
- All decisions (removal, refund amount, refund date) are logged with timestamps for audit
