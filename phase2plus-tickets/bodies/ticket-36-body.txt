## Overview
Build a public-facing marketing landing page for Poolr to drive signups, explain the product, and link to the app stores and web app.

## Sections
- Hero: headline, subheading, CTA ("Join the waitlist" or "Get started")
- How it works: 3-step explainer (Create a group, contribute each cycle, collect your payout)
- Features: key selling points (KYC-verified members, automated payouts, penalty protection, wallet)
- Testimonials: placeholder for beta tester quotes post-beta
- App store badges: iOS App Store and Google Play links
- Footer: links to Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, contact email

## Tasks
- [ ] Build landing page at `apps/web/app/(marketing)/page.tsx` or as a separate `apps/landing` Next.js app
- [ ] Use Poolr brand colors (#085041 primary, #EF9F27 accent) and the approved coin-ring logo mark
- [ ] Make fully responsive (mobile-first - most Nigerian users will land on mobile)
- [ ] Integrate a simple waitlist/signup form (Tally, or a lightweight `POST /waitlist` endpoint)
- [ ] Add SEO metadata: title, description, Open Graph image
- [ ] Deploy to production Vercel domain
- [ ] Set up Google Analytics or Plausible for basic traffic tracking

## Acceptance criteria
- Landing page is live at the production domain
- Page is fully responsive on mobile (375px) and desktop
- Waitlist form submissions are captured and stored
- Core Web Vitals scores: LCP under 2.5s, CLS under 0.1 on mobile
