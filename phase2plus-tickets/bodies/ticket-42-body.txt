## Overview
Share a portion of the interest earned on pooled wallet balances (float) with users whose money is sitting in the Poolr wallet. This rewards users for leaving payouts in the wallet rather than immediately withdrawing, and differentiates Poolr from competitors who keep 100% of float revenue.

## How it works
- Poolr earns interest on the aggregate balance held across all users' Poolr Wallets in its Paystack settlement account
- A configurable percentage of that interest is shared back with users proportional to their average daily wallet balance
- Interest is credited to users' wallets monthly as a "Poolr Interest" transaction
- The interest rate offered to users is lower than what Poolr earns (Poolr keeps a spread)
