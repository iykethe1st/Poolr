## Overview
Recruit a small, trusted group of real users for the private beta. The goal is to put real-world human behaviour through the app before public launch.

## Target beta group profile
- 2-3 complete Ajo groups (8-10 members each)
- Mix of segments: at least one market/trader group and one friend/individual group
- At least one group with real NGN contributions (even a small amount like 1,000 per person) to test real Paystack flows
- Testers should be people willing to give honest feedback, not just friends who will be polite

## Tasks
- [ ] Define beta tester criteria and write a simple onboarding brief explaining what they are testing and what to expect
- [ ] Set up a feedback channel (WhatsApp group or simple Tally/Google Form) for testers to report bugs and suggestions
- [ ] Recruit at least 2 complete groups (16-20 testers total)
- [ ] Onboard testers: walk them through registration, KYC, and group setup
- [ ] Run at least 2 full contribution cycles per group during beta
- [ ] Collect structured feedback at the end of beta on: trust in the platform, clarity of the UI, any bugs or unexpected behaviour

## Acceptance criteria
- At least 2 complete groups have completed at least 1 full cycle (all members contribute, payout is received by the correct recipient)
- All critical bugs discovered during beta are filed as GitHub issues and triaged
- A beta retrospective document is written summarising findings before public launch
