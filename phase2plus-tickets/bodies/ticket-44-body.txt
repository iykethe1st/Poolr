## Overview
Build a lightweight B2B onboarding flow for corporate HR teams and large market cooperatives who want to run Ajo groups for their members/employees at scale.

## What B2B adds over the standard product
- A verified "Organisation" entity that owns and manages multiple groups
- Bulk member import (CSV upload of employee/member list)
- Organisation-level admin dashboard showing all groups, total pooled, and payout schedule across groups
- Custom branding option (organisation logo on group invite emails)
- Flat monthly fee billing instead of per-transaction fee (negotiated per organisation)
