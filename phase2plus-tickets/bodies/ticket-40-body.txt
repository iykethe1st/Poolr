## Overview
Convert the waitlist built during the pre-launch period into active Poolr users with a launch email sequence and social media announcement.

## Tasks
- [ ] Write launch email sequence (3 emails via Resend):
  - Email 1: You're in - Poolr is live, here is how to get started
  - Email 2: 3 days later - how to create your first group
  - Email 3: 7 days later - invite your savings circle (referral prompt)
- [ ] Design email templates using React Email (consistent with Poolr brand colors)
- [ ] Segment waitlist by source if applicable (diaspora vs Nigeria, individual vs trader/corporate)
- [ ] Send launch email to full waitlist on launch day
- [ ] Prepare social media launch posts: Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp status
- [ ] Reach out to Nigerian fintech journalists and newsletters (Techpoint, Nairametrics, The Aza Report) with a press release or product brief
- [ ] Monitor signups and conversion rate from waitlist email open to completed registration in the 72 hours post-launch

## Acceptance criteria
- Launch email delivered to 100% of waitlist with under 2% bounce rate
- Conversion rate from waitlist email to completed registration is tracked
- At least one Nigerian tech media outlet covers or acknowledges the launch
